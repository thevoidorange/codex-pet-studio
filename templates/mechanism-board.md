## Mechanism QA

- [ ] The character remains recognizable at both extremes.
- [ ] Attachments remain visually continuous.
- [ ] The mechanism is readable without explanatory text.
- [ ] The mechanism does not create unintended anatomy.
- [ ] The range is large enough to matter but not so large that identity is lost.
