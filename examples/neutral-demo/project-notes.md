# Signal Seed — Placeholder Project Notes

## Inspiration

- An imagined smooth pebble that seems to pause before rolling.
- A blinking text cursor.
- The compact asymmetry of a seed shell.

These are conceptual references only. No external images or third-party assets are used.

## Desired presence

Signal Seed should feel:

- quietly curious;
- deliberate rather than sleepy;
- capable of one sudden, precise movement.

## Early visual constraints

- predominantly charcoal;
- one small high-contrast visual cue;
- asymmetric but not lopsided;
- compact enough to read at desktop scale;
- not clearly a cat, dog, bird, or human.

## Early motion constraints

- no constant bounce;
- idle motion should be infrequent;
- inspection should change orientation and position, not only eye direction;
- a sudden action should settle cleanly rather than wobble.

## Open decisions

- Is the high-contrast cue an eye, an aperture, or a seam?
- Does movement come from rolling, folding, or a hidden contact point?
- Which feature is the immutable identity anchor?
